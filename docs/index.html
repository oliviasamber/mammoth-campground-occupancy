Reserved occupancy counts nights with a booking (status Reserved) against sellable inventory (Available + Reserved). Closed, out-of-season, and non-reservable sites are excluded from both. It measures reservations, not whether the holder showed up. Week-over-week and year-over-year fill in as daily snapshots accumulate.
